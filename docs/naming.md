# Why Anneal?

The name **Anneal** comes from metallurgy.

Annealing is a process of heating and slowly cooling a material to relieve internal stress and improve its properties.

This idea reflects the purpose of the project: putting yourself through repeated interview practice, learning from each attempt, and gradually becoming stronger and more composed under pressure.

The name also connects to **simulated annealing**, an optimization technique used in computer science.

**Practice. Measure. Adapt. Repeat.**
