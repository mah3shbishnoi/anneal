# Vision

Anneal aims to make interview preparation a continuous training process rather than a collection of practice questions.

The goal is to create a place where you can practice realistic interviews, understand your weaknesses, track your progress and see how you improve over time.

Anneal is built around a simple idea:

> **Don't just prepare for the interview. Train for it.**

The long-term vision is to make interview preparation **measurable, adaptive, personal and local-first.
